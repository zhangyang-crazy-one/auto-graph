# Phase 16 Plan 01: Page Policy Rails, Gutters, And Bundles

<objective>
Execute page-level route capacity policies for dense dependency, resource-flow, lane-behavior, and IBD/high-fan-in diagrams.
</objective>

<threat_model>
No external IO. Main risks are non-deterministic policy classification and overfitting to fixtures. Mitigate with explicit policy hints, deterministic sorting, and generic capacity calculations.
</threat_model>

## Truths

- D-01: Use explicit hints when provided and deterministic classification otherwise.
- D-02: Dependency pages get top/bottom rails and rail occupancy scoring.
- D-03: Resource-flow and IBD/high-fan-in pages get side gutters and fan-in/fan-out bundles.
- D-04: Lane-behavior pages reserve lane-aware corridors and avoid header/title bands.
- D-05: If capacity is exceeded, return a split plan with counts and affected edge/node subsets.
